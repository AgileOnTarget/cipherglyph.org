Privacy Policy

Effective 21 August 2026 · CipherGlyph Viewer · Agile On Target LLC

Short version. CipherGlyph Viewer is a read-only iPhone app. It has no account, no login, no wallet, no ads, and no analytics SDK. The app reads public blockchain data and stores saved addresses only on your device.

Developer

CipherGlyph Viewer is provided by Agile On Target LLC. Privacy questions can be sent to support@cipherglyph.org.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the CipherGlyph Viewer iPhone app and the public pages at cipherglyph.org, including the App Store Privacy Policy URL and Support URL.

Data we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, Apple ID, account, or login.
  • No advertising identifier, tracking, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or crash reporting SDK.
  • No contacts. Share uses the Apple system share sheet. The app does not read your address book.
  • No private keys, seed phrases, wallet files, signing, sending, receiving, balances, or transactions from your wallet.
  • No photos from your camera roll. The camera scanner is for QR codes only.
  • No precise location.

Data stored on your device

DataPurposeRetention
Saved public addresses and optional nicknames Let you revisit a Glyph wall without pasting the address again. Stored on this device until you delete the saved address or delete the app.
Last lookup result for a saved address Show cached results when the public explorer is unavailable. Stored on this device until replaced, deleted in the app, or removed when the app is deleted.

Network requests

When you look up a BadCoin address, CipherGlyph Viewer asks the public BadCoin explorer for the known issuer address history, then filters GLY1 payloads on your device by the hash160 of the address you entered. The app does not query the scanned address as a payment address.

The BadCoin explorer is a third-party public blockchain service. Its operator may receive ordinary technical logs such as IP address, request time, and requested URL. Agile On Target LLC does not receive an account, profile, or analytics feed from that explorer.

Bitcoin and Dogecoin lookup are not configured in the app at this time. If a chain is not configured, the app stops before sending that address to an explorer.

Camera permission

The app asks for camera access only to scan a public address QR code. No photo is stored by the app or sent to Agile On Target LLC. If you deny camera access, you can paste an address instead.

You can revoke camera access at any time in iOS Settings: Settings, CipherGlyph Viewer, Camera.

Website

The website is hosted as static files on GitHub Pages. We do not run user accounts, advertising tags, or analytics tags on these pages. GitHub may process standard hosting logs under GitHub's own terms and privacy policy.

The web address lookup uses a bundled public chain snapshot today because the public BadCoin explorer does not send browser CORS headers. If a future hosted index is added, this policy will be updated before that service collects or processes additional data.

Children

CipherGlyph Viewer is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Delete your data

Delete saved addresses inside the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone also deletes the app's local saved addresses, nicknames, and lookup cache.

Because we do not operate accounts for CipherGlyph Viewer, there is no server-side account profile to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will be reflected on this page before they apply to new app behavior.

Contact

Email support@cipherglyph.org for privacy questions, support requests, or data questions.
