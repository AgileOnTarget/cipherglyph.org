WHAT CIPHERGLYPH IS

CipherGlyph is a geometric alphabet by Tom Friend. Each letter becomes one Glyph: a base shape, tick marks, and a dot on the left or the right. It is designed to be readable by people, teachable in a classroom, and reconstructable from raw chain data.

This is a teaching cipher, not a lock. Plain, Caesar, and Vigenere modes are there for learning and play. The Glyphs are meant to carry writing, not hide secrets.

TEACHER KIT

The Teacher Kit is the public learning material for CipherGlyph: lesson framing, classroom use, and the alphabet history behind the marks. It is the place to start if you want to understand the writing system before touching the chain.

BADGLYPH ON BADCOIN

BadGlyph is the BadCoin deployment of CipherGlyph. Ordinals carries pictures. BadGlyph carries Glyphs: the letters themselves, packed into GLY1. The home page lets you type a message, render the Glyphs, copy the stream, copy the payload hex, and export a receipt.

IPHONE READER

CipherGlyph Viewer is the read-only iPhone app. It scans or pastes a public address and reads GLY1 inscriptions. It is not a wallet. It has no keys, no signing, no sending, no account, and no inscription path.
